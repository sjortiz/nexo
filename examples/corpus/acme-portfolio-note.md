# Acme Holdings: 2025 portfolio note

## Acquisitions

Acme Holdings Inc. (AHI) bought Beta Labs in March 2024. Beta Labs makes rapid diagnostic kits for
respiratory infections and remains a separate brand inside AHI.

## Leadership

Jane Roe has been chief executive of Beta Labs since 2021. Before Beta Labs she led product at Delta
Devices. Investor relations contact: jane.roe@betalabs.example.

## Outlook

AHI expects Beta Labs to expand into home testing in 2026.
