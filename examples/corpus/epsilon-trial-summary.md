# Epsilon Health: respiratory kit trial

## Design

Epsilon Health ran a 2025 field trial of the rapid respiratory kits made by Beta Labs at Gamma
Clinics. Acme Holdings Inc. sponsored the trial.

## Participants

Participant Carlos Vega (MRN 44712-A) was diagnosed with asthma in 2019 and takes salbutamol. His
kit results were consistent with the laboratory reference in every visit.

## Findings

The kits matched the laboratory reference in 94% of cases. Epsilon Health recommends that Gamma
Clinics keep Beta Labs as its supplier of respiratory test kits.
