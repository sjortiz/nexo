# Delta Devices: company profile

## Overview

Delta Devices Ltd. designs wearable glucose monitors. It was founded in Cork in 2015 by Arun Mehta,
who remains its chief executive.

## Leadership history

Jane Roe led product at Delta Devices from 2017 to 2020, before she joined Beta Labs. Priya Nair has
been head of product at Delta Devices since 2021.

## Customers

Since 2025 Delta Devices supplies continuous glucose monitors to Gamma Clinics under a pilot
agreement. The pilot covers two clinics and is reviewed every six months.
